Why add a trailer

A smarter way to grow your business

Whether you run a farm, a building crew, a food stall or a fleet, a purpose-built trailer lets you do more with the vehicle you already have.

01/ 06

Lower cost than a truck

A trailer has no engine, gearbox or fuel bill. You add carrying capacity to the vehicle you already own instead of buying and insuring another one.

More work in every trip

Carry bigger loads, tools and stock in a single run. Fewer trips means less fuel, less time on the road and more time earning.

Take the business to your customers

Food, retail, events, mobile workshops and services can all run from a trailer, anywhere there are customers.

Built around your business

Racking, shelving, tanks, ramps, doors and finishes are agreed with you, so the trailer fits how you work.

Flexible and easy to maintain

Hitch it when you need it and park it when you do not. Simple, serviceable parts keep running costs low and downtime short.

A moving advertisement

A branded trailer is seen all day on the road and on site, putting your name in front of new customers for free.

Questions

Before you decide

Will my vehicle be able to tow it?

Most of our trailers suit a standard pick-up, SUV or van with a tow bar. Tell us your vehicle and the load, and we will confirm the right trailer. We also build and install towing hitches.

Can you build something that is not on the site?

Yes. Every trailer is made to order, and the custom request form is there for anything special: tanks, racking, ramps, kitchens or shop fit-outs.

What does it cost?

Each trailer shows a starting price for the base model. Your final price depends on size, options and custom work, and is confirmed in a written quote.

What do I need to do before I can use it on the road?

Register and license the trailer under local law and match it to a vehicle rated to tow it. Our Terms and notices section covers the details.

Good to know

Terms & notices

Please read these before you order or tow. They are a general guide and do not replace the written quote we give you.

Photos and specifications

Photos are illustrative. Trailers are built to order, so finishes, colours, dimensions, fittings and options can be tailored to your needs and wants, so your trailer may differ from those pictured. The final specification is agreed with you.

Pricing and quotes

Prices on this site are indicative starting ("from") prices for each base model, in US dollars. They include registration and local delivery, and exclude insurance, optional extras and custom work. Prices can change without notice. A price applies only when it is in a written quote from us, and quotes are valid for a limited time.

Example images and brands

Some images are examples or concepts of a trailer type and may not show an Evoris build. Any brand or product names mentioned belong to their owners.

Towing and load limits

The buyer must make sure the towing vehicle, tow bar and hitch are rated for the trailer, and must never exceed its rated capacity, including payload and tow ball weight.

Registration and road legality

The buyer is responsible for registering and licensing the trailer and for keeping it roadworthy under local law, including lights, reflectors and number plates.

Intended use

Use each trailer only for the purpose it was built for. Fuel and water tankers, hearse and funeral trailers, animal carriers and mobile shops may need extra permits or approvals (such as hazardous goods, food hygiene or livestock transport). The buyer is responsible for obtaining them.

Safe operation

Secure every load, and inspect and maintain the trailer regularly. A hitch or other part fitted by someone else is outside our control.

Enquiries and custom requests

Sending an enquiry or custom trailer request is not a binding order or an agreement to build. A trailer is agreed only once we confirm the details with you in writing.

Privacy

Our forms collect your name, phone, email and message. We use them only to reply to you. Forms are delivered by Web3Forms, which processes the data on our behalf. To ask us to delete your details, email evorisenterprisesltd@gmail.com.
